# Code of conduct

Hushtile grew out of cleanyourtv, where thousands of people helped each other fix their TVs. Keep it like that.

## Expected

- Be kind. Many people here are fixing a parent's or grandparent's TV, in a second language, on their first pull
  request.
- Assume good faith. A list that breaks something was tested on different firmware, not written to hurt anyone.
- Say what you tested on. Specifics ("Android TV 11, Nokia ATV R1") help; verdicts ("doesn't work") do not.
- Respect decisions on scope. If your idea does not fit Hushtile, a fork is a good outcome, not a rejection of you.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or
  Discussions.
- Publishing other people's private information: device identifiers, Wi-Fi names, IP addresses, ADB keys or anything
  that could identify a TV or a home.
- Lists or instructions that uninstall, root, or intentionally damage a TV, or that target a specific person's device.
- Spam, affiliate links, or passing off someone else's work as your own.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove content and block accounts that break these rules, with a short
explanation. To report something, open an issue, or if it is private, use the Security tab's private report as
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes. Reports are handled within a few days.

A short, plain-language code adapted from cleanyourtv's and, in spirit, from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
